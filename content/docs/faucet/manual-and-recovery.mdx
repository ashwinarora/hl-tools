Manual mode does each step of the faucet miner by hand, one wallet at a time. It is also where you get money back from the miner's wallets after a run that did not finish.

## Manual mode

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add a wallet

Under **Manual Mode**, click **Add Wallet**. A new wallet is created and its key kept in this browser.

</Step>
<Step>

### Activate it

Click **Receive $1**. You sign <Fact id="activationFee" /> and your account is debited 2 USDC: the 1 USDC the wallet receives, plus Hyperliquid's fee for a new address.

</Step>
<Step>

### Claim and drain

Click **Claim** to take the testnet faucet's drip, then **Drain** under **Drain Testnet** to send the testnet USDC to you.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get your 1 USDC back

Click **Drain** under **Drain Mainnet**. The wallet's mainnet USDC returns to you, at no fee. Each wallet costs you <Fact id="activationFee" /> in the end.

</Step>
</Steps>

**Refresh all balances** reads every wallet's balances again.

## Getting funds back

Money can be left in a generated wallet when you click **Abort**, when a step fails, or when you close the tab during a run. It is not lost while this browser keeps the wallet's key.

- **After Abort or an error**: the progress card stays. Click **Drain** next to any balance that is not zero.
- **After closing the tab**: next time you open Auto mode, a banner says how many wallets are left from a previous session and what they hold. Click **View & recover →**: Manual mode lists them, each with its **Drain** buttons.

## Deleting wallets

**Reset**, **Run Again** and **Delete all wallets** first read every wallet's balances. If any still holds money, a dialog lists them under **Delete these wallets forever?**. Click **Go back and drain first**. **I know — delete forever** deletes the keys, and any money in those wallets is lost for good.

<Callout type="warn" title="Careful">
The trash icon on a single wallet's row deletes it at once, without checking its balance. Drain a wallet before you delete it.
</Callout>

## Good to know

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Can I export a generated wallet's key?">
Not from the page. The Drain buttons are the way to get money out, so you never need the key.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Clearing browser data">
It deletes the generated wallets' keys. Drain every wallet first.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Auto wallets in Manual mode">
Wallets made by Auto mode are tagged **auto** in the table. They were activated by the run, so they show "—" under Activate.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
