Hyperliquid keeps an account's money in one of two ways, its account mode. The mode decides which ways of sending work, and every Multisig form follows it.

| Mode | Balances | Payments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Manual** | Two: perps and spot. Moving USDC between them is a **Perps ↔ spot** proposal | You choose which balance pays and which one the receiver gets it in |
| **Unified** | One balance for both | Taken from that one balance, with nothing to choose |

The treasury's Overview shows its mode as a tag, such as **unified account**, next to the balances.

## Switch the mode

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the form

On the Overview, click **Change** next to the mode tag. Or choose **Switch account mode** in **Propose an action**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the mode

The form says **This treasury's mode now: …**. Under **Switch to**, pick **Unified account** or **Manual account**. The form says what changes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create, sign, send

As with any proposal. Your wallet shows the mode in Hyperliquid's own word for it: `unifiedAccount` for unified, `disabled` for manual.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What changes

- **To unified**: the perps balance and the spot USDC become one balance. Positions and open orders stay as they are.
- **To manual**: perps and spot become separate balances again. The USDC stays in the spot balance; moving some to perps is a **Perps ↔ spot** proposal afterwards.

## Good to know

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Back to manual needs nothing open">
Hyperliquid refuses a switch back to manual while the treasury has open positions or orders. The form stops you and says so. A running TWAP order stops the switch too, and the page cannot see one.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Waiting proposals the switch would break">
Some waiting proposals only work in one mode. The form names any that would be refused after the switch. Carry them out first, or propose them again afterwards.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Already in that mode">
Hyperliquid accepts it and nothing changes. The form tells you before you create it.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The mode could not be read">
The balances say so, and the forms warn instead of guessing. Click the round arrow to read it again.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
