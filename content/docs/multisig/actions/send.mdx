Four forms move a treasury's money. Each makes one proposal that the signers approve and the finaliser sends. Open them from the treasury's page: **Propose an action**.

| Form | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Send USDC** | Pays USDC to another Hyperliquid account |
| **Send spot token** | Pays any spot token, HYPE included, to another Hyperliquid account |
| **Perps ↔ spot** | Moves USDC between the treasury's own perps and spot balances |
| **Withdraw** | Sends USDC out of Hyperliquid to an address on Arbitrum |

## Before you start

A treasury in a **manual** account has two balances: perps and spot. One in a **unified** account has one balance, so it has no **Perps ↔ spot** form and nothing to choose about balances. The treasury's mode is shown on its Overview: see [Account mode](/docs/multisig/actions/account-mode).

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>

### Fill in the form

- **Destination**: an address on Hyperliquid itself. An exchange's deposit address is not one.
- **Taken from** (manual account): the perps or the spot balance, with what each holds.
- **Amount**: signed exactly as typed. **Max** fills in the most Hyperliquid would take now, less any fee paid from the same balance.
- **Arrives in** (manual account): the receiver's perps or spot balance.

For **Send spot token**, choose the **Token** first. **Withdraw** asks for an address on Arbitrum (Arbitrum Sepolia on testnet).

</Step>
<Step>

### Read the cost line

Under the form, one line says what the send costs:

- An address **in use** on Hyperliquid: nothing.
- A **new** address: <Fact id="activationFee" />, charged once by Hyperliquid. It comes from the treasury on top of the amount, so the receiver gets the full amount.
- A **withdrawal**: the bridge keeps <Fact id="withdrawalFee" /> out of the amount. An amount of <Fact id="withdrawalFee" /> or less is refused.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the finaliser and create it

Pick who sends it under **Who finalises this?**, then **Create and sign as 0x…**. The preview beside the form shows exactly what every signer will sign.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Your co-signers sign it, and the finaliser [sends it to Hyperliquid](/docs/multisig/send). The finished payment appears in the **On Hyperliquid** tab, tied to its proposal.

## Good to know

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Amber lines on the form">
They warn without blocking. For example: the destination is the zero address, or an API wallet, or a proposal paying the same amount to the same place is still waiting. Read them before you create.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What the form refuses">
A send from the treasury to itself, and a send to a HyperEVM system address. Hyperliquid refuses both from a treasury.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Max fills in nothing">
Max needs the figures it depends on: the treasury's mode, and for a send, whether the destination is new. Until they are read, it fills in nothing and says why.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Withdrawals go in order">
Hyperliquid takes withdrawals in order of their dates. Once one goes through, older waiting withdrawals are refused and must be proposed again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Amounts are never rounded">
What you type is what is signed. Figures on screen are cut down, never rounded up, so a balance never looks bigger than it is.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
