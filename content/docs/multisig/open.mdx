The [Multisig start page](/multisig) offers four ways in. Which one you need depends on whether you sign in and whether you are a signer.

| You want to | Use | Wallet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See your treasuries and what needs you | **Sign in with your wallet** | Yes |
| Open a proposal someone sent you, or propose without signing in | **Open or start a proposal** | To sign |
| Turn an account into a treasury | **Create a treasury** | Yes |
| Look at any treasury's signers, balances and history | **Open the multi-sig inspector** | No |

## Sign in

Click **Sign in with your wallet** and approve one message in your wallet. It proves which wallet you are. It approves nothing and cannot move funds.

Signed in, the start page becomes your inbox, **Needs you**, and the side column lists **My treasuries**. Several wallets can stay signed in in one browser: choose one in your wallet to use it, with no new signature.

## Add a treasury you sign for

A treasury created here is added to its signers' lists by itself. For one created elsewhere, click **+ Add an existing one** in the side column, paste the treasury's address and click **Check on testnet** (or mainnet). If your wallet is one of its signers, click **Add to my treasuries**.

## Open a proposal from a link or a file

Click **Open or start a proposal**. Under **Open a proposal**, paste the link or the proposal's text and click **Open**, or click **Upload file**. A link opens the proposal directly too. The proposal is decoded in this page.

Proposals you made or opened are listed under **Recent in this browser**.

## Propose without signing in

Under **Start a proposal**, paste the **Treasury address**, click **Check on testnet**, then **Propose an action →**. **Lab treasury (testnet)** fills in a demo treasury to explore with.

## Look at any treasury

**Open the multi-sig inspector** shows any treasury's signers, approved API wallets, balances, open orders and recent actions. It needs no wallet and changes nothing. See the [Multisig Inspector](/docs/tools/multisig-inspector).

## Good to know

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Which network a page is on">
A Multisig link carries its network. Opening a link for testnet switches the page to testnet, whatever the header showed. Changing the header afterwards moves the page to the other network.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="'This address is not a treasury on testnet'">
The address is a normal account on that network. Check the network in the header: a treasury on mainnet is not one on testnet.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="'Your wallet is not one of its signers'">
Only signers can add a treasury to their list. Switch your wallet to one of its signers, or ask a signer to share proposals with you as links.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Hiding a treasury">
In a treasury's **⋯** menu, **Hide from my list** removes it from your list in this browser. Your co-signers' lists do not change. **Show in my list again** brings it back.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
