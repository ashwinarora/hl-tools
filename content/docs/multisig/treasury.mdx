Each treasury has its own page, open to its signers once they sign in. The top shows the treasury's name and address, its network, and how many must sign, like **2 of 3 signers**. **Propose an action** starts a new proposal.

## The figures at the top

The tiles above every tab show what the treasury holds: its **Balance**, what **Can be moved now**, what is **In use** by open positions and orders, and what is **In staking** and **In vaults**. A manual account shows its perps account value and spot USDC separately.

Balances, positions and orders arrive live from Hyperliquid as they change. There is nothing to reload.

## The tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Balances, positions, outcome shares and open orders. The account mode, the **Signers** card and the **API wallets** card |
| **Pending** | Proposals not yet carried out, with the number waiting in the tab's name |
| **Staking** | HYPE in spot, in the staking balance, staked and on its way back. See [Staking](/docs/multisig/actions/staking) |
| **Vaults** | Each deposit's worth, profit, and when it can be withdrawn. See [Vaults](/docs/multisig/actions/vaults) |
| **Signing log** | What happened here: who proposed, signed, said no and sent |
| **On Hyperliquid** | Hyperliquid's own record: transfers, trades, orders and funding, whoever caused them |

## Signing log and Hyperliquid's record

These are two records. The **Signing log** explains what happened here. **On Hyperliquid** is the proof.

In **On Hyperliquid**, each outgoing transfer is tied to the proposal that caused it. **None on record** means no proposal known here explains it: it was signed and sent another way, for example with another tool. If none of the signers knows it, find out who signed it.

Wherever a proposal reads as accepted, the page checks it against Hyperliquid's record. That record covers the account's newest 300 transactions.

## Exporting

**Download CSV** saves the Signing log or a table of **On Hyperliquid**, with times in UTC and every figure to its last decimal. **Export as a file** saves the Signing log as JSON.

<Accordion title="Nicknames">

<Accordion title="Hiding a treasury">
In the **⋯** menu next to the name, **Hide from my list** removes it from your list in this browser, with **Undo**. Your co-signers' lists do not change.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Outcome shares">
Shares of a prediction outcome are listed under the Overview's **Outcomes** tab, never among the balances.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="'Hyperliquid's signer list differs from the copy kept here'">
Someone changed the signers in a way this page has not caught up with. Click **Ask again** to read the list from Hyperliquid.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Times">
Times are in your own time zone. Hover over one to see it in UTC.
</Accordion>
