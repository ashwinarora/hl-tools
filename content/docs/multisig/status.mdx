The top of every proposal page says where the proposal stands. This is what each status means.

## Still open

| Status | Means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Checking signatures…** | The page is checking each signature against the signers Hyperliquid lists. | Wait a moment. |
| **Collecting signatures · 1 of 2** | It needs more signatures. The line says who can still sign, and how long the signing window has left. | Signers: [sign](/docs/multisig/sign). |
| **Ready · 2 of 2 signatures** | Enough have signed. | The finaliser: [send it](/docs/multisig/send). |
| **Cannot be sent yet** | It is signed, but Hyperliquid would not take it yet, for example an unstake inside its lockup. | Wait until the time it shows. |
| **Waiting on you** | Your no is what holds it up. | Take back your no, or leave it. |
| **Cannot pass as things stand** | Too many signers said no for it to reach the number needed. | Talk to your co-signers, or let the proposer cancel it. |

## Finished

| Status | Means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sent · accepted by Hyperliquid** | Done. | Nothing. |
| **Carried out, not reported here** | Hyperliquid carried it out, but it was sent another way, for example while signed out. | Nothing. It was closed so nobody signs it again. |
| **In effect** | A signer change that is already live. | Nothing. |
| **Already used · it most likely went through** | Hyperliquid had already taken its number. | Check **On Hyperliquid** before proposing anything again. |

## Ended without going through

| Status | Means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Refused by Hyperliquid · cannot be sent again** | Hyperliquid said no, and its number is used up. The reason is shown. | Fix the cause, then **Propose it again**. |
| **Cancelled by the proposer** | The proposer took it off everyone's list. | Nothing. Signatures already given stay valid until the window closes. |
| **The finaliser will not send it** | The finaliser said no. | The proposer can propose it again with another finaliser. |
| **Expired** | Its signing window closed before it was sent. | **Propose it again**. |
| **Cannot be sent any more** | A newer payment of a kind that goes in order was sent first. | **Propose it again**. |
| **Cannot be finished** | Its finaliser is no longer a signer. | **Propose it again** with another finaliser. |
| **Closed** | The proposal was closed on the hl-tools server. | Nothing. |

## When the page cannot judge it

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Not a treasury on testnet** (or mainnet) | The address is not a multi-sig on that network. Check the network. |
| **Signers not loaded** | The signer list could not be read from Hyperliquid yet. The page tries again. |
| An action this page cannot sign | A trading action, which a treasury makes through an [API wallet](/docs/multisig/actions/api-wallets), or a form of action hl-tools has not tested. |

## Good to know

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="'Propose it again' is a new proposal">
It opens the form filled in with the same action and a new proposal number. Signatures do not carry over: everyone signs again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Notes under the status">
Anything that must not be missed is shown in full under the status. Everything else is folded into **N notes**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The timeline">
Each proposal page has a timeline: when it was proposed, signed, said no to and sent, from the hl-tools server's history or from the copy's own dates.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
