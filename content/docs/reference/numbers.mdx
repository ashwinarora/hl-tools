## Where these come from

Some figures were measured on testnet by hl-tools; others come from Hyperliquid's documentation where they could not be measured, such as mainnet waits of several days. The rule files say which is which, and Hyperliquid can change any of them. When a rule changes, hl-tools updates its rule set, and the change is listed on [Where the rules come from](/docs/reference/rule-sources).
